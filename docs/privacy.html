Privacy Policy – Poor Dave

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Poor Dave ("we") is an independent creator project that publishes short animated videos on TikTok (@poor.dave) and YouTube (@poordavewhatif). This policy describes how our publishing tool handles data when it connects to TikTok and YouTube through their official APIs.

What the tool does

The tool uploads and schedules our own videos to our own accounts. It does not interact with other users' accounts or content.

Data we access

Through the TikTok Content Posting API and the YouTube Data API we access only: the authorization tokens for our own accounts, basic profile information of our own accounts (display name, avatar, privacy options), and the status of videos we upload. We do not collect, store or process personal data of viewers or of any third party.

Storage and retention

Authorization tokens are stored encrypted on our private infrastructure and are used only to publish our content. Tokens are deleted when the connection is revoked. We keep no viewer data.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

YouTube API Services

Our tool uses the YouTube API Services to upload and schedule videos to our own YouTube channel. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data accessed through YouTube API Services is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

From YouTube we access only: the ID, title and handle of the connected channel (to confirm the right channel is connected) and the upload status of the videos we send. We store the authorization tokens encrypted and nothing else. We do not use cookies or similar technologies on our tool to collect data from YouTube users.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the tool's access to your Google/YouTube account at any time on the Google security settings page. When access is revoked, the stored YouTube tokens stop working and we delete them within 7 days; channel information we saved is deleted at the same time. You can also ask us to delete any stored data by writing to vizamapi@gmail.com.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) or Google Account permissions (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Google API Services usage complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
