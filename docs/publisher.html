Poor Dave Publisher Private tool for the @poor.dave account

1. App settings (owner only, stored in this browser)

The Client key of the Poor Dave Publisher app (it is public) and a GitHub access token that lets this page start our publisher job. The TikTok client secret and the account tokens are never in this page: they live encrypted in our GitHub publisher job, which talks to TikTok.

2. Connect TikTok account

Sign in with TikTok to connect the Poor Dave account. We request: user.info.basic (to show which account is connected), video.upload and video.publish (to send our own videos to this account).

YouTube channel

Sign in with Google to connect the Poor Dave YouTube channel (youtube.upload to schedule our own Shorts, youtube.readonly to show which channel is connected).
