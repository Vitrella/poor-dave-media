It's Saturday, and Dave has done the big shop.
Four bags, one door, and Priya's wedding list. Item one: eggs.
Then everyone on Earth doubles in height. For five seconds.
One point seven metres becomes three point four. Doors are two. Ceilings are two point four.
Eight billion people. All of them. At once.
Everyone indoors is now partly upstairs. Priya is in the loft.
Weight goes up eight times. Dave now weighs five hundred and sixty kilos. Like a polar bear, but anxious.
Bones only get four times stronger. Every bone now carries double the load. Galileo worked this out in 1638. Nobody listened.
A living room floor is rated for a hundred and fifty kilos per square metre. A family of four now weighs two tonnes.
An eight-person lift now holds one person. Barely.
One point four billion cars. Zero drivers fit. Every sunroof is now a hat.
Clothes don't double. Eight billion outfits fail at once. Dave's jumper is now a crop top.
Your heart now pumps blood an extra thirty-five centimetres uphill. Giraffes manage. Giraffes have been practising.
Dave tries the door sideways. Then the window. Then the door again.
Then everyone shrinks back.
Heads come down out of ceilings, sunroofs and lofts. The holes stay.
Dave gets through the door. Item one did not. Priya writes it down again.
