It's Sunday at Nan's house.
Dave is opening Nan's pickles for the wedding. She made them in nineteen ninety-eight. The lid has not moved since.
Then everyone on Earth becomes ten times stronger. Only the muscles you control. Your heart is excluded. It has been through enough.
An average grip is forty-seven kilos. Now it's four hundred and seventy. Dave puts the jar down. Very slowly.
A normal jump is forty centimetres. Now it's about seven metres. Everyone who was skipping is now on a roof.
Everyone pulling a door right now gets to keep the door. The hinges were not consulted.
Your bite is about eight hundred and fifty newtons. Now it's eight and a half thousand. That's a small car, standing on one tooth. Toast has never been less of a problem.
Here's the catch. Your bones and tendons stay exactly the same. Your muscles could already break your own bones. Your brain stops them. For five seconds, your brain is very busy.
Nan's grip was nineteen kilos. Now it's a hundred and ninety. She takes the jar. She does not ask.
Then the strength goes back.
Eight billion people put everything down, very carefully. Roofers are suddenly very busy.
The jar is open. Dave says he loosened it for her. Nan does not comment.
